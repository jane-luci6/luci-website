One platform. One embedded team.

LUCI is The Orchestration Engine for Enterprise Multimedia. Systems is the embedded operation around the platform: six capabilities, available when needed and built into every deployment. One accountable team helps you scope, design, stand up, operate, and refine LUCI for the life of the relationship.

Forward-deployed engineers stay close from early scoping through the ongoing relationship.

Reach LUCI FDE when you are weighing a multimedia system, an addition, or an expansion—before assumptions harden into a budget. The embedded team can be a sounding board for design, planning, and budget questions while working alongside the architects, vendors, and trades already involved.

That support mindset begins as the engagement begins. The same team remains involved as the work moves toward go-live and into operations, preserving context from day one through the life of the relationship.

Design & Deployment

Rich

Outline

  • Infrastructure planning and design included in the subscription relationship
  • Scope from tenant improvements to larger additions
  • One design thread across architecture, systems, trades, commission, and training
  • Budget from plans before bid; refine after approval
  • Phased stand-up and go-live with the same team

Draft

Design begins with how you operate, then carries one accountable thread from plans and budget through commission and training.

Planning and design for the infrastructure LUCI needs are built into the subscription relationship. The scope can range from a single restaurant tenant improvement to a larger addition, and from an LED-only package to broader low-voltage and LED design, depending on the project.

Starting with the architect’s drawings, LUCI plans landing rooms, places racks and nodes with the intent to minimize hardware, defines speaker chains and groupings, and coordinates requirements with other trades. Pathways remain responsive to field conditions. You can bid hardware installation to others while the LUCI team stays connected through design review, stand-up, commission, go-live, and owner training.

Budget planning follows the same thread. Building a budget from plans before bid gives you a package and working numbers before a deeper commitment. After approval, the team can refine the scope, guard against overbuilding, and phase the work so operational value arrives earlier.

Draft

Your LUCI FDE works on your side of the table as a standing extension of the enterprise.

The embedded team brings operational context to roadmap, vendor, capital, and guest-facing technical decisions. It checks plans against your standards and helps keep the scope aligned to what the operation needs—not overbuilt around a disconnected specification.

Because the same team carries the work from plans to budget and bid, accountability does not disappear when other vendors sell or install the equipment. LUCI stays connected to the technical decisions and the operating outcome.
