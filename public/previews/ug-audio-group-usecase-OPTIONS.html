Proposed customer paragraph

Keep the room’s balance as volume changes

Your dining room can stay quieter than the bar while the patio stays louder—even when all three need to come up or down. In the old LUCI, moving several zones together meant setting them to the same volume, flattening the balance you had tuned.

New LUCI lets you move selected zones together while keeping those differences intact. You can still set every selected zone to one volume when that’s what the room needs, or adjust a single zone without moving the rest.

Three ways to move volume

Choose the change the room needs

01

Keep the mix

Raise or lower selected zones by the same step. Dining can stay quieter than the bar while the patio stays louder.

02

Set them equal

When selected zones need one shared volume, set them all to the same level at once.

03

Move one alone

Fine-tune one zone—such as the patio—without changing the dining room or bar.
