G — Radial (The Call Comes to You)
Four stages arranged in a circle around a central phone/alert — "the call comes to you." Each stage radiates from the center, suggesting the cyclical nature: silos create blind spots, vendors can't help, and it all comes back to your phone. Non-linear, experiential.
The Silos A/V runs on separate, proprietary systems — each one a black box you can't see into.
The Blind Spot You can't monitor what's running, what's exposed, or what's failing.
The Vendors Each system came with its own vendor — and none of them talk to each other.
The Page When any of it fails, it's your phone that rings — and you have no way to diagnose it.
