Four stages stacked vertically, each with its icon on the left and text on the right. Background tint darkens with each stage — the weight builds as the story progresses. Works well on mobile.
The Silos A/V runs on separate, proprietary systems — each one a black box you can't see into.
The Blind Spot You can't monitor what's running, what's exposed, or what's failing — because it's not on your network.
The Vendors Each system came with its own vendor, contract, and support line — and none of them talk to each other.
The Page When any of it fails, it's your phone that rings — and you have no way to diagnose it.
