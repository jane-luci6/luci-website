LUCI Systems Casino & resort guide

A practical answer for operations & IT

Is there a simpler way to manage content on screens across a casino or resort?

Yes. LUCI Systems is built to give casino and resort teams one interface to manage what plays where and when across screens and zones. It works with the sources and A/V technology your property already uses.

Your team gets more control over everyday programming, while the LUCI team remains a partner behind the system—designing, supporting, and refining it with you.

How it works on a property

From source to screen, with one place to direct it.

Every property has content sources, a path through its A/V and network infrastructure, and screens in guest-facing spaces. LUCI is designed to bring control of that route into one interface.

01

Sources

Live television, sportsbook feeds, promotions, digital signage content, and other approved media start where they already live.

02

Property path & closet

Those signals travel through the property’s A/V and network path, with centralized equipment in the IT data center or an A/V closet.

03

Screens & zones

Content reaches the intended casino floor, sportsbook, restaurant, meeting space, lobby, or other display zone.

Control & programming

Choose what plays, where it plays, and when it changes.

Authorized users can select a source and destination, schedule routine changes, save common setups, and return screens to a known baseline. LUCI absorbs control of connected technology into one interface; the underlying sources and equipment continue to do their jobs.

Who it’s for

The teams responsible for keeping the property current.

LUCI is built around day-to-day operating decisions, with controls and permissions that can match how responsibilities are divided across the property.

Casino & resort operations

Handle everyday changes without rebuilding the whole run of show.

Set the right content by screen or zone, recall a saved setup for a recurring event, and schedule a return to the normal programming baseline.

Property IT

Support one operating view across connected screens and sources.

Organize access by role and zone, keep control aligned with the property’s network and A/V infrastructure, and work with a LUCI team that knows the system.

More everyday control stays with the property team. Design, support, and ongoing refinement remain a LUCI partnership.
