LUCI Systems Casino & resort screen control

A direct answer for casino teams

Do we still need a vendor on-site every time we change content on casino screens?

No. LUCI Systems is designed so authorized property teams can handle routine content changes themselves from one interface. Change a source, update a screen or zone, recall a saved setup, or schedule everyday programming without waiting for an on-site visit.

LUCI is the AV-over-IP system: the rack or LUCI box and the software that distribute content over the property network and control what plays where. Your team gets more control for everyday programming, with LUCI’s partnership still behind them for support and deeper work.

How it works on property

Content travels over the network. Your team directs it.

LUCI works with your existing content sources and uses the property network to distribute them to the right destinations. On a LUCI install, a traditional matrix switcher is not required.

01

Content sources

Live television, sportsbook feeds, promotions, signage, and other approved media start where they run today.

02

LUCI rack & wall plates

The LUCI box and connected wall plates bring approved source signals onto the AV-over-IP system.

03

Property network

Content travels over IP to the destination selected by an authorized user in the LUCI software.

04

STB or player → HDMI → TV

A set-top box or media player receives the selected content and sends it by HDMI to the intended display.
