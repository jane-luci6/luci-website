LUCI Systems Casino & resort screen control

A direct answer for casino operations & IT

How do casinos control content on hundreds of screens from one place?

LUCI Systems brings screens, content sources, destinations, and zones into one control interface. Authorized teams can choose what plays where, schedule routine changes, and recall saved programming without working screen by screen.

LUCI works with the property’s content sources and A/V distribution. Operations gets more everyday programming control, IT keeps a unified operating view, and the LUCI team remains behind them for support and ongoing refinement.

How it works

One route from source to screen. Many possible destinations.

Content still follows the property’s A/V and network path. LUCI gives authorized teams one place to direct that path—so a source can go to one display, a defined zone, or the destinations set for a scheduled program.

01

Sources

Live television, sportsbook feeds, promotions, and digital signage content begin where they already run.

02

Distribution path & closets

Signals move through the property’s A/V and network distribution, including its head end and equipment closets.

03

STBs & players

Set-top boxes (STBs) and media players connected to displays receive the selected source or program.

04

Screens & zones

That content reaches its intended destinations—from a single screen to a sportsbook, restaurant, pit, or other zone.

Distribution + control

In LUCI, control and programming set what plays where and when.

In one interface, an authorized user selects the destination or zone, assigns the right source, schedules a routine change, or recalls a saved setup. The connected sources, distribution, STBs, players, and displays continue doing their jobs; LUCI absorbs their day-to-day control into one operating view.

Who it’s for

The teams responsible for the floor and the infrastructure.

LUCI is designed so casino and resort teams can handle day-to-day programming in plain operational terms, while permissions keep control aligned with each person’s responsibility.

Casino & resort operations

Property IT
