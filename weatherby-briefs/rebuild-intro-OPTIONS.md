# Rebuild intro options

For Jane’s pick. Each option is written to run unchanged immediately after either locked callout:

- Upgrade Guide: “The only A/V that scales and improves is about to get even better”

## A — Built from your feedback

We heard you. The new LUCI was rebuilt from the ground up around what our clients asked for: stronger capabilities, better performance, and easier upgrades. The LUCI Hub connects your systems to live monitoring, chat, and instant support—putting more control in your hands while keeping the LUCI team close.

**Leans on:** Heard you; capabilities and performance; easier upgrades; Hub, monitoring, chat, and support.

## B — Connected support

Your feedback shaped the new LUCI from the ground up. We invested in the capabilities, functionality, and performance that make LUCI better and easier for the entire LUCI family. The LUCI Hub connects your systems to live monitoring, chat, and instant support—giving your team more control and ours better context to support you.

**Leans on:** Heard you; capabilities and performance; family-easier; Hub, monitoring, chat, and support.

## C — Better together

We listened to what clients needed next, then rebuilt LUCI from the ground up to improve its capabilities, functionality, and performance. Support, monitoring, and upgrades are easier across the LUCI family, with the LUCI Hub connecting your systems to live monitoring, chat, and instant support—and the LUCI team still behind you.

**Leans on:** Heard you; capabilities and performance; family-easier; Hub, monitoring, chat, and support.

## Recommendation

**A — Built from your feedback.** It is the shortest and most direct dual-use lead: it starts with Mike and Jane’s “we heard you” beat, gives a clear reason for the rebuild, and folds the Hub into the partnership story without reading like a feature list.
